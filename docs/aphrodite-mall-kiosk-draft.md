## Plywood decision

The user authorized using 40 sheets provisionally in this draft, not confirmed production usage. The inference comes from 60,000 / 1,500 = 40 and the separate “40” appearing in the source row. Preserve the source's conflicting Units value of 15 until NJL confirms the correction. At 15 sheets, the corresponding plywood cost is PHP 22,500 and the adjusted material total would be PHP 155,977.
